The story in one line

A woman who lived with PMDD for years before it had a name taught herself to build the women's health app she couldn't find: for all conditions, with your data kept on your phone.

Three ways to tell it.

Believed.

Too many women haven't been believed at the appointment. The app turns weeks of symptoms into one clear, doctor-ready report, so she can walk in with trust and confidence.

Private by design.

Health apps ask for a lot of trust. This one is built so it doesn't need yours: entries stay on the phone, and we never receive them.

Every body.

For all conditions, and made to fit each person: 17 colours, dyslexia-friendly fonts, four text sizes, higher contrast, larger tap targets and Listen.

The founder.

Sarah Hughes lived with PMDD for years before she had a name for it. With no development background, she taught herself to build The Veda Circle from scratch, because she couldn't find a women's health app that felt like it was actually built for her. She is building it from Malta.

I believe every woman should be able to walk into her doctor's appointment with trust, confidence, and power. Too many women haven't been believed. It's time to change that.Sarah Hughes, founder

For creators.

If you share about health, wellbeing or life with a cycle, you are welcome to try The Veda Circle as a gift. There is no script, and no obligation to post.

What you get

  • Full access to everything in the app, as a gift.
  • The pictures, logos and founder photos on this page, in full resolution.
  • A reply from Sarah herself, and an answer to any question about the app.

What we hope for

  • Your honest opinion, including anything that did not work for you.
  • A clear note that the app was gifted.
  • Your own colour and settings on show, because making it yours is the point.

What people often find worth saying

  • Entries stay on the phone and we never receive them.
  • One doctor-ready report to take to an appointment.
  • 51 conditions across every stage of life, made to fit each person: 17 colours, four reading fonts, four text sizes, and a choice of more pictures or more words.
  • It was built by one woman in Malta who lived with PMDD for years before it had a name.

How to ask

Write to hello@thevedacircle.com with the subject “Creator access”. Tell us your name, where you share, and whether you use Android or iPhone. Android is available now. If you use an iPhone, we will write to you as soon as the iPhone app is ready.

A gift never depends on what you say, and we never ask anyone for a store review in return.

If you're sharing it.

Please

  • Say clearly if a post is paid or gifted.
  • Describe it as a way to track and understand your body, and to prepare for appointments.
  • Show your own colour and your own settings. That is the point.

Please don't

  • Say it diagnoses, treats or cures anything, or calls itself medical advice. It doesn't.
  • Share anyone else's health information in a post.
  • Say your data is private in ways we haven't: entries stay on the phone, and only an anonymous subscription check goes online.

Journalists and creators are welcome to full access. Write to us and we will set you up.
