About

Hi, I'm Sarah.

Why I'm building this

I taught myself to build The Veda Circle from scratch, with no development background, because I couldn't find a women's health app that felt like it was actually built for me. Not just another tracker, but a tool for everyday life, at every stage of it. Everything in one place, in a single subscription. An app that actually supports you.

I believe every woman should be able to walk into her doctor's appointment with trust, confidence, and power. To trust her own body, and to be able to explain how she feels and what's happening. Too many women haven't been believed. It's time to change that.

What guides this app

Built for every body

The Veda Circle is designed from day one to work for women across every disability, culture, and walk of life. Dyslexia-friendly fonts, colour-blind modes, high contrast, and full screen reader support are built in, not bolted on.
