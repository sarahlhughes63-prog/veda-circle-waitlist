Six questions worth asking of any health app

How it's different.

Is my condition included?
51 conditions across every stage of life, and you can add your own.
Where does my data live?
On your phone. We never receive your entries, so we can't read, sell or share them.
Can I take it to my doctor?
Yes. One doctor-ready report for the dates you choose, to share, print or save as a PDF.
Can I make it work for me?
Yes. 17 colours, 4 reading fonts including dyslexia-friendly ones, 4 text sizes, higher contrast, larger tap targets, and Listen to hear a screen read out loud.
Is it in my language?
Eight languages, including Maltese.
Will it show me adverts?
No. There are no adverts, and nothing in the app is built to sell your attention.

Around pregnancy and birth

For every stage, and the people beside you.

Guides and tools for the weeks before and after birth, for talking with the people who support you, and for the words you meet along the way. Information only, not medical advice.

Postpartum

The fourth trimester. A gentle space for your recovery, counted from the day of the birth or the day your pregnancy ended.

  • Recovery guides, from the first days to the first year
  • Simple records of bleeding, sleep, mood and feeding
  • Health visitor and lactation support, and when to seek help

Preparing for birth

Plan calmly. Pack thoughtfully. Know what to look for.

  • Your birth preferences, which you can change any time
  • Packing lists for you, for baby and for your birth partner
  • A notebook for your questions, and PDFs for your midwife

Talking to your partner

Words for the people who want to help. Write a short note in your own words about where you are, how you feel, what helps and what doesn't.

  • Guidance for partners, from pregnancy and your cycle to postpartum and after menopause
  • You read the whole note before anything is shared
  • Share by Messages, Mail or copy, only when you choose
