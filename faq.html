Frequently asked questions

Real answers to the things people actually ask before joining.

When does The Veda Circle launch?

We're launching on 5 November 2026, across the EU and UK, on both iOS and Android.

How much does it cost?

€13.99 a month, flat. There's a 14-day free trial so you can try it before you're charged anything.

Is my health data really private?

Yes. The app is built so your health data (cycle, symptoms, mood, journal entries, photos) never leaves your phone. There's no cloud backup and no account required for any of it, so there's nothing stored anywhere else to access.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. Since your health data never leaves your device in the first place, there's nothing for us to sell or share.

Is the moon-cycle tracking feature required?

No, it's entirely optional and switched off by default. You can turn it on in Settings if it's something you'd like.

I've joined the waitlist. What happens next?

We'll email you the moment doors open on 5 November 2026, before we announce it anywhere else.
