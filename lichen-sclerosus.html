What lichen sclerosus is

Lichen sclerosus is a skin condition that can cause itching, soreness and pale patches, often in the vulval area. It is long-term but manageable, usually with treatment from a healthcare provider.

Why a record helps

Symptoms can come and go. A short daily note of how much you itch gives you something concrete to show a clinician, and lets you see whether things are settling or getting worse.

What you can record in The Veda Circle

  • How much you itched today
  • Anything else you noticed, in your own words
  • Photos of symptoms, if you want them, saved only on your phone

Taking it to your appointment

The doctor-ready report is built from your own entries for the dates you choose. Share it, print it or save it as a PDF.

When to get help

Itching or soreness that does not settle, pale or thin patches of skin, or skin that splits or hurts are all worth having looked at. Because treatment usually comes from a healthcare provider, it is worth seeing a doctor rather than waiting.
