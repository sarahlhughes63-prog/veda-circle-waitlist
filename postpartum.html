What postpartum recovery is like

Recovery after birth takes longer than most people are told. Bleeding, pain, tiredness, mood and feeding all change from day to day. The Veda Circle counts the weeks from the day of the birth, or the day your pregnancy ended.

Why a record helps

A daily record of how you are really feeling, and not just how things look from the outside, is one of the most useful things you can bring to a midwife, health visitor or GP. Mood changes after birth are common. They range from the ordinary baby blues to something that needs real support, such as postnatal depression or anxiety.

What you can record in The Veda Circle

  • How your body feels today
  • Your mood, and your own words when you want to add them
  • How much you slept over the last day
  • Anything else you want to remember, such as questions for your midwife
  • Recovery guides by stage, from the first days to the first year, and a place for health visitor and feeding support

Taking it to your appointment

The report is built from your own entries for the dates you choose. Share it, print it or save it as a PDF, so your midwife, health visitor or GP can see how you have really been.

When to get help

Some things need help the same day: heavy bleeding that soaks a pad in an hour, a fever, a painful swollen calf, chest pain or breathlessness, a severe headache or changes in vision. If you ever feel unsafe, or have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, contact your midwife, GP or a crisis line straight away. It does not need to wait for an appointment. If you are in danger or need urgent help, call your local emergency number: 112 in Malta and across the EU, or 999 in the UK.

Common questions

How long does postpartum recovery take?

It takes longer than most people are told, and it is different for everyone. The app has recovery guides from the first days through the first year.

What is the difference between baby blues and postnatal depression?

Mood changes after birth are common and range from ordinary baby blues to postnatal depression or anxiety, which need real support. If you are worried about how you feel, speak to your midwife, health visitor or GP.

Does The Veda Circle replace my midwife or doctor?

No. It gives general information and tools so you can prepare for appointments. It does not diagnose or treat anything.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your entries are saved on your phone. We never receive them, so we cannot read, sell or share them.
