Frequently asked questions

Real answers to the things people actually ask before they begin.

When does The Veda Circle launch?

The Veda Circle launches on 5 November 2026 on Google Play, across the EU and the UK. The iPhone app is coming soon: leave your email on the home page and we will send one message the day it is ready.

How much does it cost?

Every plan starts with 14 days free. After the free trial you pay €13.99 every month, or €39.99 every 3 months, or €74.99 every 6 months, or €99.99 every year. The longer plans work out cheaper per month: €13.33, €12.50 and €8.33. In the UK the same amounts are charged in pounds, and other countries see their own currency in the store. Plans renew automatically, and you can cancel any time in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with 14 days free, so you can try The Veda Circle before you pay anything. The free trial is for new subscribers, and Google Play or the App Store decides whether you are eligible. You can start it from the plans on the home page.

How do I cancel my free trial?

Open your Google Play or App Store subscription settings, choose The Veda Circle and cancel. You do not need to contact us. To be safe, cancel at least a day before the 14 days end.

Will I be charged after the 14 days?

Only if you stay. You are not charged during the trial. Unless you cancel before it ends, your chosen plan starts automatically and Google Play or the App Store charges your payment method at the price shown. Plans then renew automatically until you cancel.

Is my health data really private?

Yes. Your entries (cycle, symptoms, mood, journal and photos) are saved on your phone. We never receive them, and there is no cloud copy. You can save a backup file yourself if you want one. The only thing that goes online is an anonymous check that your subscription is active.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. Because your health entries stay on your phone, we do not have them to sell or share.

Does it cover my condition?

All of them. The app covers 51 conditions across every stage of life, including PMS, PMDD, perimenopause, menopause, endometriosis, PMOS (formerly PCOS), adenomyosis, fibroids, ADHD, autism, anxiety, POTS and lichen sclerosus, and you can add your own. Life stages run from fertility and pregnancy to postpartum and after menopause.

Can I take my information to my doctor?

Yes. The app builds a doctor-ready report from your own entries for the dates you choose, so you can share it, print it or save it as a PDF.

Is The Veda Circle medical advice?

No. It gives general information and tools to help you understand your body and prepare for appointments. It does not diagnose or treat anything and is not a substitute for your doctor.

Which languages is the app available in?

English, Malti, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Русский and Eesti.

Is the moon-cycle tracking feature required?

No. It is optional and switched off by default. You can turn it on in Settings if you would like it.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is coming soon. Leave your email in the iPhone list on the home page and we will send you one message the day it is ready.

Does it have a planner, a journal and reminders?

Yes. The planner is built by you: choose pages such as to-do and priorities, shopping, bills, budget, meal plan and a calendar, and add your own. The journal has no streaks, with prompts as suggestions only. Reminders cover daily check-ins, medication and vitamins, at the times and on the days you choose, and stay off until you turn them on.

How is my email stored if I sign up for updates?

Your email is stored securely in an EU-based database in Ireland, run for us by our database provider, Supabase, and in MailerLite, the email service we use to send our updates. We use it only to send you updates about The Veda Circle, including the day the iPhone app is ready. We never sell or share it, nothing about your health is connected to it, and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time by writing to hello@thevedacircle.com.

Is The Veda Circle accessible?

It is built with accessibility in mind: four reading fonts including Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic, four text sizes, higher contrast, larger tap targets, reduced motion, 17 colours, Listen to hear a screen read aloud, and labels for screen readers. This website has the same choices, and you can try them on the home page, plus line and letter spacing, underlined links, a reading guide, a pause for movement and Listen. We have not had a formal external audit yet. The accessibility page lists everything, including what we are still improving. Read about accessibility.

Does it cover pregnancy, birth and postpartum?

Yes. There is a postpartum guide for the fourth trimester, with recovery guides by stage and simple records of bleeding, sleep, mood and feeding. There is also a preparing-for-birth section with birth preferences, packing lists and a notebook for your questions, both with PDFs you can share with your midwife. It is information only and not medical advice. You can also write a note for your partner in your own words, and look up any term in the plain-language dictionary.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@thevedacircle.com and we will answer. Ready when you are: try it free for 14 days, or look inside the app first.
