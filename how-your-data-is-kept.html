How your data is kept.

In plain words: what is stored, where, and what leaves your phone.

The short version

Everything you log stays on your phone. We never send it to ourselves, to a server, or to a cloud backup of our own. We cannot read it, sell it or share it, because we never have it.

What is saved on your phone

  • Your cycle, symptoms, mood and sleep
  • Your journal entries and symptom photos
  • Your medications, condition logs and reminders
  • Your planner, habits and anything else you add

What leaves your phone

One thing only: a small check that your subscription is active. It sends an anonymous app-generated ID, your store purchase receipt, your device type and the app version. Your device’s IP address is visible to the service that receives it, as with any internet request. No health entries are included. The check is handled by RevenueCat, a subscription service, which asks Apple or Google and gets back a yes or no.

What we do not have

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No name or email from you inside the app
  • No adverts, and nothing in the app is built to sell your attention
  • No tracking of what you do inside the app

Your choices

  • Delete any entry, photo or the whole app and it is gone, because we hold no copy
  • Save a full backup file yourself, kept wherever you choose, which we never receive
  • On an iPhone, iCloud backup is controlled by your device settings and handled by Apple. On Android, the app asks Android not to include its data in your Google backup

This website

If you sign up for updates, we store only your email address, in a secure EU-based database in Ireland and in MailerLite, the email service we use to send our updates, to send you updates and one email when the iPhone app is ready. We have switched off email open tracking. This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Our host, Vercel, receives your visit as any web host does, and we use its cookieless counter to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It stores nothing on your device. Nothing else is loaded from other companies. Your colour, text size and font choices are saved in your own browser and never sent to us.

The full policy

This page is the plain-words version. The full wording, including your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR, is in the Privacy Policy. Questions are always welcome at hello@thevedacircle.com.

Where is my health data stored?

On your phone. Your cycle, symptoms, mood, journal, photos and everything else you enter are saved on your device. We never receive them.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account or sign-up, so we cannot identify you as a person from your use of the app.

What leaves my phone?

Only an anonymous check that your subscription is active. It contains no health content and no name or email.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an entry, a photo, or the app itself. Because we hold no copy, deleting from your phone removes it completely. If you saved a backup file yourself, or have iCloud backup switched on, delete that too.
