The Veda Circle App

Privacy Policy · Effective Date: 23 October 2026 · Version 1.1

1. Who We Are

Sarah Lesley Hughes, trading as "The Veda Circle" ("The Veda Circle", "we", "us", "our"), a sole trader established in Malta and holding Malta VAT number MT33031516, operating from Malta, publishes The Veda Circle mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what little personal data is involved in using the App, why, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR together with the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. The Short Version

Everything you log in the App (your cycle, symptoms, mood, sleep, journal entries, photos, medications, condition logs, and anything else you enter) stays on your device. We never send it to ourselves, to any server, or to any cloud backup of our own. There is no account or sign-up, so we have no way to identify you as a person from your use of the App.

One thing to be aware of: your phone may back itself up (for example to iCloud or a Google backup) if you have that switched on in your device settings. If it does, the App's data on your phone may be included in that backup. That is controlled by your device settings and handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. It is not something the App or we do.

The one exception to the App's offline design is a narrow, specific check needed to confirm your subscription is active, described precisely in Section 3. That check never includes any health content and is not tied to your name, email, or any identity we hold.

3. The One Network Call the App Makes: Subscription Verification

To offer paid subscriptions, the App uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription-management service, to check whether your subscription is currently active and to load the available plans and prices. This check sends:

  • An anonymous, app-generated user ID (not your name, email, or any account identifier, because none exists)
  • Your device's App Store or Play Store purchase receipt or transaction data
  • Your device platform (iOS or Android) and the App's version number
  • As with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the service that receives the request

RevenueCat forwards the receipt to Apple's or Google's servers to validate it and returns a yes or no "is this subscription active" result to the App. This is the only data that leaves your device on the App's own initiative, and the only reason it does so. No cycle data, symptoms, moods, journal entries, photos, medications, condition logs, or any other content you log is included in this call, sent to RevenueCat, or sent anywhere else by the App. The App has no network-sending code path for that data; the billing check is technically separate from everything else in the App.

We do not collect your name or email as part of this check. Separately, when you subscribe, Apple or Google handle your purchase and payment details directly as part of their own standard in-store checkout. That transaction is between you and Apple or Google, and we do not collect or see your payment details, name, or email through it.

Some actions are started by you and are not part of the App's own activity. These include opening a link in your browser, sharing or exporting a report through your device's share sheet, or making a phone call from the App's support screens. What happens to information you choose to share is then governed by the app or service you share it with.

4. Your Health Data: We Are Not Its Controller

Because the App never transmits anything you log, we never receive, store, view, or process your health and wellness data in any form. Any processing of that data, such as creating, editing, or deleting it, happens entirely on your device, under your control, using the App as a local tool. We do not act as a data controller or processor for that content, because it never reaches us.

This means the rights described in Section 9 (access, portability, erasure, and so on) are not something you need to ask us for in relation to your logged health content: you already have full, direct, immediate control over it on your device. Deleting an entry, a photo, or the App itself removes it from your device completely, because we hold no copy. If you have enabled a device backup (see Section 2), a copy may remain in that backup until you delete it there.

5. Apple Health (Optional, On-Device Only)

If you choose to, and only with your explicit permission granted through your device's own permission system, the App can connect to Apple Health (iOS) to read your step count, sleep data, and exercise data, and display it alongside your logged content. This includes data from third-party wearables and devices, such as smart rings, watches, or fitness trackers, that sync into Apple Health on their own. The App only reads from Apple Health. It does not write or change any data in Apple Health.

The App does not connect to any wearable, ring, or device manufacturer directly, and does not use any device maker's own API or cloud service; it only reads from the operating system's own health store, regardless of which device originally produced the data.

This data is read and stored only on your device. Exactly like everything else you log in the App (see Section 2), it is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent anywhere by the App: not to us, not to any server, not to Apple, and not to Google. It is simply a further on-device data source the App can read from, at your instruction.

You control this feature entirely. The App only requests access to Apple Health if you actively choose to connect it, and you can disconnect it, or revoke the App's access, at any time through your device's Health app or settings. Disconnecting does not automatically delete data already read into the App on your device; like any other logged content, you can delete it within the App at any time.

If a Health Connect (Android) option is added to the App in future, this Section will be updated before it is offered.

6. Other On-Device Features

The following features of the App work on your device and do not send your information to us:

  • Voice input: if you tap the button to speak instead of typing, your phone's own speech recognition turns your voice into text on your phone. The button is only shown on phones that can do this on the device. Your voice is not recorded or uploaded by the App.
  • Read-aloud: your phone's built-in voice reads the screen back to you. Nothing is sent anywhere to make this happen.
  • Reminders and notifications: reminders you turn on are created and delivered on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.
  • App Lock and Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: unlocking is handled by your device. The App does not receive or store your biometric data.
  • Symptom photos: photos you take within the App are kept in the App's own storage on your device, not in your camera roll or photo library, and are never uploaded.
  • Sharing and exporting: reports and summaries you choose to create are produced on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it, and then only to the person or app you choose.

7. Automated Processing and Predictions

The App shows predictions (such as your likely next period date) and patterns in your own logged data. These are produced on your device by fixed, rule-based calculations (calendar arithmetic and simple pattern rules) using only what you have entered. They do not use machine learning or generative AI. We make no decision about you based on this processing, and it produces no legal or similarly significant effect for you. It is not a diagnosis and it is not a method of contraception. See also our AI Transparency Policy.

8. Legal Basis for the Subscription Check

The limited data described in Section 3 is processed on the basis of contractual necessity (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and is necessary to provide the paid Subscription you have chosen to purchase and to verify your entitlement to continued access.

9. Your Rights

Subject to conditions and exceptions under applicable law, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of any personal data we hold, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In practice, given Sections 4 and 5, this applies only to the narrow subscription-verification data described in Section 3 and to any email address you give us through our website (Section 16).

Even the subscription-verification data is not linked to your identity on our side, so in most cases there is nothing further for us to look up, disclose, or delete beyond what RevenueCat itself may hold against the anonymous ID. You can contact RevenueCat directly, or ask us to make that request on your behalf, at hello@thevedacircle.com.

If you are in the EU, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Malta Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC), or with the supervisory authority of your EU member state of residence. If you are in the UK, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Who We Share Data With

  • RevenueCat (subscription and receipt validation service) receives the anonymous ID, receipt data, platform, app version, and the technical information described in Section 3.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) receive and validate purchase receipts, and separately process your purchase as your own store transaction. For their own store-level purchaser records they act as independent controllers, and we do not collect or see those records.

Data read from Apple Health, where you choose to connect it, is not included in anything described in this Section. It stays on your device and is not shared with us or with any third party. See Section 5. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs in the App.

11. International Data Transfers

Subscription-verification data described in Section 3 may be processed outside the EEA or the UK as follows:

  • RevenueCat (USA): protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs, Module 2: Controller-to-Processor) under GDPR Article 46 and the UK GDPR, as documented in RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum (effective August 2026, available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat's sub-processors are listed in that Addendum. RevenueCat maintains SOC 2 Type II certification.
  • Apple and Google (USA): receipt validation takes place under Apple's and Google's own developer terms and data processing terms, which apply to registered developers.

12. Data Retention

We do not receive and therefore do not retain your logged health content. Subscription-verification data is retained by RevenueCat and the app stores under their own retention practices; we do not separately store a copy. Email addresses given through our website are kept until you ask us to remove them or unsubscribe.

13. Security

Because your health data does not leave your device through the App, it is not exposed to a breach of our systems, our infrastructure, or any third-party processor we use: there is nothing centrally stored to breach. Your device's own security (passcode, biometric lock, operating-system updates, and any App Lock you switch on) is what protects your logged content, and we recommend keeping these enabled.

14. Children and Age

The App is intended for adults aged 18 or over (or the age of legal majority in your country if higher), as set out in our Terms and Conditions. It is not directed at children. The App collects no personal information beyond the anonymous subscription check described in Section 3.

15. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event that RevenueCat or another processor notifies us of a personal data breach affecting the limited data described in Section 3 that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it (GDPR Article 33), and notify affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk (Article 34), to the extent we are able to identify and reach them.

16. Our Website and Waitlist

Our website (thevedacircle.com) is separate from the App. If you join the waitlist or newsletter, we collect the email address you give us, only for that purpose. It is stored in an EU-based database in Ireland. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and a script library from jsDelivr, which means those providers can see your IP address when the page loads. You can ask us to remove your email address at any time at hello@thevedacircle.com. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, in particular if the App's design changes to introduce any account system, sync, voice assistant integration, or additional network activity. We will notify you of material changes by in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.

18. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@thevedacircle.com, or in writing to Sarah Lesley Hughes, trading as "The Veda Circle", Malta. If you or your organisation email us (for example about sponsored access or feedback), we use your email address and what you tell us only to reply and to administer that request, and we do not share it.
